Inside the calculation

First-year EPS bridge

Lucite runs a first-year accretion/dilution bridge — the standard way a deal's earnings impact is estimated before it closes. Four steps take the terms on the left to the verdict on the right.

  1. 01
    Purchase price

    The offer takes the target's share price and adds the premium. Multiplied by the target's shares, that is the total equity price the acquirer pays — divided into stock, cash, and debt by the consideration sliders.

  2. 02
    New shares

    The stock portion is divided by the acquirer's share price to find how many new shares it issues. These add to the existing count, so a stock-heavy deal dilutes the buyer's earnings more.

  3. 03
    Pro forma earnings

    Both companies' net income combine, plus after-tax synergies, less the after-tax interest on new debt and the after-tax yield surrendered on cash spent.

  4. 04
    The verdict

    The combined earnings per share are set against the acquirer's standalone EPS. Higher is accretive; lower is dilutive. As a rule of thumb, an all-stock deal adds to earnings when the buyer trades at a higher multiple than the one it pays for the target.
