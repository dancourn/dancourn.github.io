Live financials were pulled from Yahoo Finance and seeded into the assumptions. This is a starting point, not a finished model. A few things to keep in mind:
↗
Revenue growth is seeded from TTM — adjust it to your forward estimate, not just last year's number.
%
EBIT margin reflects the most recent trailing period. If the company is in a turnaround or growth phase, today's margin isn't the right assumption.
$
Net debt is sourced from balance sheet data that Yahoo occasionally overstates. Cross-check against the latest 10-Q if the output looks wrong.
⚡
WACC is estimated via CAPM with Blume-adjusted beta. It's a reasonable starting point — but cost of capital is a judgment call, not a lookup.
